## Kinetrell

[![Kinetrell Duo by Shalfey Predskazatel](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Shalfey-Predskazatel/Kinetrell/main/assets/social-preview.png)](https://github.com/Shalfey-Predskazatel/Kinetrell/releases/latest)

Портативный видеоплеер для Windows с NVIDIA VFG, RIFE, RTX Video Super Resolution и HDR. Доступны редакции VFG, RIFE и Duo; Maxine включён в VFG и Duo.

**[Скачать](https://github.com/Shalfey-Predskazatel/Kinetrell/releases/latest)** · [Исходный код](https://github.com/Shalfey-Predskazatel/Kinetrell) · [Сообщить о проблеме](https://github.com/Shalfey-Predskazatel/Kinetrell/issues)
